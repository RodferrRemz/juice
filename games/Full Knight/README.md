# Full Knight

**Rodrigo Antunes Ferreira:**

**How would you pitch your game in one line?**

Hollow Knight inspired, a small game that tries to mimic the magic of this amazing game.
 
**What is the key mechanic(s) that make it fun?**
I intend to make the story very good, and make the gameplay engaging with a unique and challenging system like Hollow Knight pulls off so perfectly.

**How does the game actually play? (Core Game Loop)**
The player will run around the world of Azaure with a very dark but hopeful theme to it, defeating enemies and bosses and getting upgrades along the way.

**How will you keep players engaged for 30 minutes? What's the general scope of your game (will change as you go)?**
The game will frequently give you new ways to experience the combat and to explore the world of Azaure with more ease, like dashes and double jumps, or spells for fighting.
